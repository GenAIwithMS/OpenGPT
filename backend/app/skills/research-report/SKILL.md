# Research report

## Gather

- Use several independent sources. Prefer primary sources (official docs,
  papers, filings, data) over summaries of them.
- Note the date of each source; flag anything that may be out of date.
- When sources disagree, keep both views and say which is better
  supported.

## Structure

1. **Summary** - three to five sentences answering the question directly.
2. **Key findings** - short paragraphs or bullets, most important first.
   Each claim cites its source inline as a markdown link.
3. **Comparison** (when comparing options) - a table of the criteria that
   matter, followed by a recommendation and why.
4. **Caveats and open questions** - what is uncertain, missing or
   contested, and what would resolve it.
5. **Sources** - the links used.

## Style

- Lead with the answer; put background after it.
- Plain language; define terms the reader may not know.
- Numbers with units and dates. No claims without a source; say "I could
  not confirm" rather than guessing.
